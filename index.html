Research

ISMIR 2026

Mitigating structural collapse in long-horizon music generation

Multi-codebook music models like MusicGen fall apart late in long generations: rhythm loosens and timbre drifts. I showed this happens because small errors compound across both time and codebook depth. Structure-Aware Scheduled Sampling (SASS) trains the model on its own predictions, replacing whole frames at once so the codebooks stay consistent with each other.

  • 5.6–10.0%lower KL divergence than teacher-forced MusicGen
  • 14.3%better beat regularity, with no loss in text–audio alignment
  • A/Bpreferred by listeners over teacher forcing and the 1.5B InspireMusic
  • Sametokenizer, architecture and inference speed as MusicGen

Under review, AISTATS 2027

Correlated tasks in multi-task world model distillation

When one teacher world model is distilled from several related tasks, the student often does worse than with a single-task teacher. I showed this negative transfer can come from the teacher averaging over the tasks’ dynamics, which gradient-conflict diagnostics miss. Across 28 Meta-World task pairs, its cost tracked confusability-weighted dispersion (r = 0.90).

COMET, the teacher I designed, uses orthogonalized experts and a dynamics head per source task. It matched or beat separate single-task teachers on four out-of-distribution targets with 3.5× fewer parameters.
